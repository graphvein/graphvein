# CLA privacy notice

> **Version 1.0, effective 2026-11-26.** Applies once the Graphvein Contributor License
> Agreement is signed through CLA Assistant.

**What is recorded.** When you sign the Individual Contributor License
Agreement, CLA Assistant records your GitHub user name, the date you
signed, and the version of the Agreement text you agreed to. If you give a
name or e-mail address when signing, that is recorded too.

**Why.** Only to show that the license in the Agreement was granted for
your Contributions, by whom, and under which text.

**Where it is kept.** In CLA Assistant's records for the Graphvein
repository, and in copies the Project Owner keeps as evidence of the
Agreement. Your GitHub user name and the fact that you signed are visible
on your pull requests.

**Removal.** You can ask the Project Owner to remove anything that is not
needed to prove the license, for example an e-mail address you gave. Send
the request to contact@graphvein.com, or privately through the repository's
private reporting form (see `SECURITY.md`), and start the subject or title
with "CLA data:". The user name, date
and text version are kept for as long as the Project uses your
Contributions, because they are the proof of the license.

**No other use.** This information is not used for anything else: no
marketing, no profiling, and it is not sold or shared beyond what is
described here.
